## Overview

The IMSystem Platform is our flagship product - a comprehensive web platform for managing programming services, client projects, and business operations.

## Features

- **Project Management** - Track project progress, deadlines, and deliverables
- **Client Portal** - Give clients real-time access to their projects
- **Invoicing** - Generate and send invoices directly from the platform
- **Team Collaboration** - Internal messaging and task assignment
- **Analytics** - Real-time dashboards and reporting

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Infrastructure**: AWS, Docker

## Results

Since launching the platform, our clients have seen:
- 40% improvement in project delivery time
- 60% reduction in communication overhead
- 99.9% uptime
